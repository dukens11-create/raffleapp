🎫 Manual Payment Instructions

Complete your raffle ticket purchase using MonCash or NatCash

MonCash Payment

Send payment to:
+509 3666 2371

📱 Step-by-Step Instructions:

1
Dial USSD Code
On your Digicel phone, dial *202#
2
Select "Send Money"
Choose the option to send money to another user
3
Enter Merchant Number
Type: +509 3666 2371
4
Enter Amount
Enter the exact amount for your tickets (shown in your order summary)
5
Confirm with PIN
Enter your MonCash PIN to complete the transaction
6
Save Transaction Reference
You will receive an SMS with a transaction reference number. Save this number!
7
Submit Reference Below
Enter the transaction reference in the form below to verify your payment
⏱️ Verification Time: Your payment will be verified by our admin team within 24 hours. You will receive an SMS confirmation once approved.

6
