# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: February 19, 2024

## Introduction

Grate Genyen ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Phone number (for account creation and authentication)
- Name and email address (for seller accounts)
- Payment information (processed through third-party payment gateways)
- Profile photos (for seller verification)

### Usage Information
- App usage statistics
- Device information (model, OS version)
- IP address
- Location data (if permitted)

### Ticket Information
- Purchased tickets
- Transaction history
- QR code data

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain our services
- Process ticket purchases and payments
- Verify seller identities
- Send notifications about tickets and raffles
- Improve app functionality
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure token storage
- Encrypted local storage for sensitive data
- Regular security audits

### Data Retention
- Account data: Retained while account is active
- Transaction data: Retained for 7 years (tax compliance)
- Cache data: Cleared after 30 days

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Payment Processors**: MonCash, NatCash
- **Analytics**: Firebase Analytics
- **Crash Reporting**: Firebase Crashlytics
- **Cloud Storage**: Firebase Storage

These services have their own privacy policies.
