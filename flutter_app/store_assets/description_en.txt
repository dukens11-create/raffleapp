### How It Works:

1. **Sign Up**: Create your account with your phone number
2. **Browse**: Explore available raffle tickets
3. **Select**: Choose your tickets and add them to your cart
4. **Pay**: Complete payment using MonCash or NatCash
5. **Confirm**: Receive instant confirmation and digital tickets
6. **Track**: Monitor your tickets and await draw results
