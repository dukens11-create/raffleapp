# Privacy Policy for Grate Genyen

**Last Updated**: February 19, 2024

## Introduction

Grate Genyen ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect the following personal information:

- **Account Information**: Phone number, full name, email address (optional)
- **Transaction Information**: Payment details, ticket purchases, transaction history
- **Profile Information**: User preferences, department/location (for sellers)
- **Device Information**: Device ID, operating system, app version

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: App interactions, features used, time spent
- **Device Data**: IP address, device type, mobile network information
- **Location Data**: Approximate location (with your permission)
- **Camera Access**: For QR code scanning (with your permission)

### Payment Information

- **MonCash/NatCash**: We do not store full payment card details
- **Transaction Records**: Payment amounts, dates, and status
- **Payment Methods**: Which payment methods you've used

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information for:

1. **Service Provision**
   - Process ticket purchases
   - Handle payment transactions
   - Manage your account
   - Provide customer support

2. **Communication**
   - Send transaction confirmations
   - Notify you about draw results
   - Respond to support inquiries
   - Send important updates about the service

3. **Improvement & Analytics**
   - Analyze app usage patterns
   - Improve user experience
   - Fix bugs and technical issues
   - Develop new features

4. **Security**
   - Prevent fraud and abuse
   - Protect against unauthorized access
   - Verify user identity
   - Comply with legal obligations

5. **Marketing** (with your consent)
   - Send promotional offers
   - Notify about new raffles
   - Share updates and news

## How We Share Your Information

### We DO NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with:

1. **Service Providers**
   - Payment processors (MonCash, NatCash)
   - Cloud hosting services
   - Analytics providers
   - Customer support tools

2. **Legal Requirements**
   - When required by law
   - To protect our rights
   - In response to legal requests
   - To prevent fraud or harm

3. **Business Transfers**
   - In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale
   - You will be notified of any such change

4. **With Your Consent**
   - When you explicitly agree
   - For purposes you authorize

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted using SSL/TLS
- **Secure Storage**: Personal data is encrypted at rest
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal data
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and updates
- **Secure Authentication**: Token-based authentication

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
2. **Correction**: Update or correct your information
3. **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and data
4. **Portability**: Request your data in a portable format
5. **Objection**: Object to processing of your data
6. **Restriction**: Request limitation of data processing
7. **Withdraw Consent**: Opt-out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@grategenyen.com

## Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as:

- Your account is active
- Needed to provide services
- Required by law
- Necessary for legal claims

After account deletion, we may retain some data for:
- Legal compliance (e.g., financial records)
- Fraud prevention
- Backup purposes (deleted within 90 days)

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at: privacy@grategenyen.com

## Cookies and Tracking

Our app uses:

- **Analytics**: To understand app usage (Firebase Analytics)
- **Crash Reporting**: To fix bugs (Firebase Crashlytics)
- **Performance Monitoring**: To improve app performance
